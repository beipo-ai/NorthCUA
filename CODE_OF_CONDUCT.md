# Community conduct

NorthCUA welcomes users and contributors with different backgrounds and levels
of experience. Keep project discussions respectful, constructive and focused on
the work. Explain disagreements with evidence, and help others report problems
without sharing private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces. Do not post
credentials, private screenshots or personal task data in issues or pull requests.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[zhangchaoyun@qijizhifeng.com](mailto:zhangchaoyun@qijizhifeng.com) or
[libingxuan@qijizhifeng.com](mailto:libingxuan@qijizhifeng.com). Include the relevant
context and links, with sensitive details removed. Maintainers may edit or
remove harmful content, close a discussion or restrict participation as needed.
Reports will be handled with care for the privacy of the people involved.

For software vulnerabilities, follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
